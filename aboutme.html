Christine Z

about me.

Hi, my name is Christine! Some friends call me CZ, some friends know me as @xtinezhao.

I grew up in many places, but I call sunny Northern California my home.

Currently, I'm a student at Stanford University, in the heart of the Bay Area.

Art has always been a part of my life. Through music…

Through photography…

Through pottery, a lifelong hobby…

And now through design of digital and physical spaces!

I'm a firm believer that design can and should be used for good in this world.

My background in psychology has greatly informed my interest in designing for mental health and community.

In this increasingly digital age, I believe that in-person experiences are the key to alleviating loneliness.

Looking into the creation of third-spaces, CPGs, and UI/UX design to solve these problems.

Outside of my professional pursuits, I'm a fiend for a good meal…

A frequent enjoyer of live music…

A quality-time-aholic…

And always in pursuit of new experiences.

In my near future, I hope to pursue a creative career in New York City, and…

Never stop learning.
